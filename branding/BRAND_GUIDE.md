# Kinevoa

**Movement, recognised.**

Kinevoa turns a live phone-camera view into an exercise label. The identity should feel like a focused movement instrument: human, observant and precise.

## Name and language

Pronunciation: kin-eh-VOH-ah. Write **Kinevoa** in prose and **kinevoa** in the visual wordmark. Avoid KineVoa, KINEVOA AI and a trailing full stop as part of the logo.

Primary tagline: “Movement, recognised.”
Functional descriptor: “Exercise recognition from your phone.”
Demo opening: “Kinevoa takes a live view from your phone camera and displays the exercise name.”

The tagline describes the product concept. It does not claim that the current image classifier validates a full movement sequence.

## Symbol

The K sits inside four viewfinder corners. The vertical stroke anchors the mark while the diagonal strokes suggest a change in position. The corners connect it to the camera's field of view.

Use the wordmark for introductions and the symbol for app icons and compact surfaces. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the symbol's width around the lockup. Minimum recommended symbol size: 24 px. Use the wordmark at least 160 px wide. Avoid stretching, rotation, gradients and shadows.

The SVG wordmarks contain editable text using Arial/Helvetica. Font metrics may vary between devices. The symbol and app icon use vector paths and do not depend on fonts. Outline the wordmark text in a vector editor before commercial print production.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #18241E | Wordmarks, primary text and actions |
| Lens | #151C19 | Camera backdrop |
| Chalk | #F2F2EB | Main light surface and inverse text |
| Signal | #BBF5B3 | Identity on dark surfaces and live status |
| Muted | #68726B | Secondary text on Chalk |
| Guide | #D6DCD3 | Quiet dividers |
| Error | #974130 | Error messages on Chalk |

Use Ink on Chalk and Chalk on Ink for most content. Signal works on Ink or Lens. Do not use Signal text on Chalk. Errors need explanatory text as well as color. Check contrast again if changing colors, opacity or text size.

## Typography

App and brand pages: Avenir Next, with Avenir and Arial fallbacks. Portable PowerPoint: Arial. The wordmark uses bold lowercase lettering with tight spacing. Keep body copy comfortably spaced. Avoid all-caps paragraphs.

Exercise labels dominate the app screen. Keep supporting instructions short. Do not compete with the label using scores, charts or promotional text.

## Voice

Use direct descriptions: “Start camera”, “Keep your whole body in view”, “Camera stopped”. Treat Unknown as a normal system output. Explain recovery steps when something fails.

Avoid medical promises, guaranteed recognition, “perfect form”, and claims of on-device inference. The current server processes camera frames. Do not imply robotic camera control is part of this demo.

## Photography and demo footage

Show a single participant with the entire movement visible, including hands and feet. Use an uncluttered room with enough separation between clothing and background. Keep camera placement consistent during a take. Present recorded footage as recorded. Use real interface captures for evidence.
